How it works

  1. Find a room

    Search classrooms, laboratories and meeting rooms by building, capacity and equipment.

  2. Request a time

    Choose a date and time and say what the room is for. The facilities office reviews every request.

  3. Track the decision

    See whether each request is pending, approved or rejected, and cancel it if your plans change.

Facility details are available after you sign in. Accounts are for Kyungdong University students.
