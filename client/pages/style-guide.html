A plain .table scrolls sideways inside its frame on small screens. Lists of records use .table--stack (below, as on My bookings): under 768px each row becomes a card, and each cell shows its column name from a .table__label. Resize the window to see it.

Tabs

Built by createTabs in js/components/tabs.js. Arrow keys move between tabs, Home and End jump to the ends, and Tab moves on into the panel. Two per row on phones, so no tab is ever off screen.

Schedule items

Bookings on the facility week (js/components/week-schedule.js) and the administrators' day schedule. Every item names its time and what it is in words; the colour and outline only support it. A pending request is always outlined with a dashed border, because it holds no time. Sample content only.

Loading, empty and error states

Rendered by renderLoading, renderEmpty and renderError in js/components/feedback.js.

Confirmation and toast

Consequential actions ask first; results are confirmed with a short toast. confirmDialog only asks. reasonDialog adds a text box: optional (the "Cancel" buttons in the table above) or required (rejecting a request, where confirming with an empty reason shows an error inside the dialog).
