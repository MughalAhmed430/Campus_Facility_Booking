Facility Usage Reports

Key figures for the bookings dated in a range you choose: how many were requested, how many were approved, and how much of their opening hours the facilities were booked for.

Date range

The date of the booking, in Korea time.

Included. At most one year after “From”.

Key figures

How these figures are calculated

Booking requests
Every request for a date in the range, whatever became of it.
Approval rate
Requests that were approved, as a share of the requests an administrator decided (approved or rejected). A booking that was completed, or cancelled after it was approved, still counts as approved. Pending requests, and requests cancelled before a decision, are left out.
Hours booked
The length of every approved and completed booking, added up.
Utilization
Hours booked, as a share of the hours the facilities were open: each facility's daily opening hours, times the days of the range it is open (Saturdays and Sundays are skipped for a facility that is closed at weekends).

A simplification to know about. Utilization uses each facility's opening hours and status as they are today, because the system keeps no history of earlier hours, closures or public holidays. Inactive facilities are left out of the total. A facility under maintenance is counted, since it is expected back, so time spent closed for maintenance lowers its figure. Read utilization as an estimate, not an audited number.
